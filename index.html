Bigger packs are up to 50% cheaper per coin, but no pack says so. Two upgrades (5k→10k, 25k→50k) give no discount at all.

What the game already has

  • Star Quest (lvl 30): a timed star bar. Wins earn stars, win streaks earn more, and milestones pay unlimited lives, boosters and coins (e.g. 350 stars → 150 coins)
  • Collection album (lvl 40) with Daily Packs for 7 / 15 / 30 first-try wins. A coin continue still counts as a first-try win.
  • Store: 6 coin packs, 3 bundles, No Ads (₺399.99), No Ads bundle

What it doesn't

  • A first-purchase or starter offer
  • Any offer or RV at the fail screen; continue costs 900 coins only
  • Single boosters for sale; any social layer
  • A use for the "battery" points earned after each level
Key insight

The strongest emotional moment in the game is failing while a streak or Daily Pack progress is at stake. The game's only answer is "pay 900 coins". Most non-payers hold 300–900 coins, so for them that moment ends the session.

Question 2

Borrow Royal Match's Teams, as lightweight "Crews"

Pick a top-grossing puzzle game, name a monetization feature Hole People doesn't have: how it works, why pick it, how to adapt it, the risks, and how to validate it.

Why Teams

Royal Match was 2025's highest-grossing puzzle game (~$1.4B IAP). Hole People already has a streak (Star Quest) and an album (Collection). The one big system missing is social.

  • Unlocks at level 21, right at the first difficulty spike
  • Out of lives? Ask the team; teammates send lives with one tap (capped)
  • Weekly team battles on a shared score; team gifts make purchases social

Why it works

  • Reciprocity: "they sent me a life, I'll send one back" brings players back daily
  • Softens the churn moment: running out of lives becomes a social action instead of a quit
  • LiveOps platform: team races, chests and gift offers become possible
  • Revenue through lifetime: more retained days means more fail moments and offers

Question 3

ARPDAU fell 10% with flat DAU: find which part of revenue moved

Over two weeks ARPDAU dropped 10% while DAU stayed flat. Which breakdowns first, what hypotheses, which teams, what action for each finding?
ARPDAU = IAP ARPDAU + Ad ARPDAU IAP ARPDAU = payer conversion × ARPPU Ad ARPDAU = Σ formats ( impressions per DAU × eCPM / 1000 ) impressions per DAU = sessions/DAU × ad opportunities/session × show rate

Every hypothesis maps to one term of this tree, so each check has a number that confirms or kills it. Flat DAU does not mean the same players: a shift in who plays can move ARPDAU while every segment is healthy.

Additional observation

Any level can be brute-forced, so players rarely fail

Problem

Tapping a blocked group has no cost: it only shakes. Players can try every option until one works. Fewer fails means less demand for continues, boosters and lives, the moments where puzzle games earn.

Idea, to test separately

On Hard levels only, give 3–5 mistakes. When they run out: +1 via RV, or +3 for coins (a new coin sink). Optional "Hard mode" with bigger rewards. Casual players dislike penalties, so retention is the main guardrail.
